No program changes. The gateway, its adapters and its verifier behave as in `v0.8.0`; only the documents changed. They now say what a `decisionPolicy` that sets `reviewed` without `packs` admits, which the `v0.8.0` notes left unsaid.

## What an operator must do

- **Nothing, to keep things as they are.** Configurations, stores and receipts are read and written as under `v0.8.0`.
- **If a policy should hold a write to particular reviewed packs,** list them in `packs`. `reviewed` alone does not.

## What `reviewed` admits (#197, closes #196)

The `v0.8.0` notes said that `reviewed` requires the cited record to say `"reviewed": true`. That is what it checks, and it is less than it may seem. The runtime writes `"reviewed": true` when the pack evaluated is in the reviewed-set lock that is current at the time. A pack that is edited and then locked again therefore yields records that say `"reviewed": true`, under a new pack digest, and a policy without `packs` admits them.

So `reviewed` holds a write to whatever lock is current when the record is written, not to the bytes someone reviewed. `packs` is what holds a write to particular reviewed packs. `SPEC.md`, the README and [ADR-0011](https://github.com/Judgment-Pack/judgment-pack-gateway/blob/v0.8.1/docs/adr/0011-hold-a-write-to-its-decision.md) now say so. The gateway does not warn about such a policy when it loads one: it has no channel to do so.
