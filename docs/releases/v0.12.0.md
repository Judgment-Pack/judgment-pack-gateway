A receipt now names the checkpoint witness its trail is registered with (ADR-0014, accepted and delivered in the gateway, rows 2 and 3): an engine configuration of version 7 may carry `witnessedBy`, one witness per trail, and every receipt minted for such a trail then carries a signed `witness` member of `origin`, `keyId` and `trail`. `gateway verify` and verify-ts hold the member to its form and report `witnesses`, and `gateway witness fetch` reads a named witness's chain through the runtime's `audit verify --witness`, from a decision record and its receipts alone. A witness's operator may now name the operators who administer it (`witness.operators`, #229), and ADR-0013 is amended for what v0.11.0 shipped. A gateway that names no witness mints receipts byte-identical to v0.11.0's, and nothing that started under `v0.11.0` refuses to start.

## What an operator must do

- **Nothing**, unless a gateway is to name a witness or a witness is to name its operators. No receipt, registry, connection or witness format changes: a version 3 receipt for a trail with no witness configured is byte-identical to v0.11.0's (a golden test re-mints every version 3 receipt of the corpus through a gateway naming a witness for another trail, with no trail and with an unconfigured one).
- **To name a witness**, raise `engine.json` to `engineVersion` 7 and add `witnessedBy`: an object keyed by trail identity, each value `{origin, publicKey}`, the witness's key pinned from the witness's operator as a verifier pins one. `gateway serve` without a configuration takes `--witnessed-by TRAIL=ORIGIN,PUBLICKEY`, repeatable. The engine refuses to start on a trail, origin or key out of form, a key the key rule refuses, a trail given twice, the member beside version 2 receipts, and **the gateway's own key**, compared by bytes: a gateway whose key the operator can use is no witness against the operator. A change takes a restart, and receipts minted before it keep naming what was configured then.
- **A request names the trail.** `/acquire` takes an optional `trail` and is refused `400`, before any source runs, when no witness is configured for it; `/act` reads the `trail` member of the record it finds. No request names a witness or a key.
- **To name the operators**, add `witness.operators`, a list of `{issuer, subject}` pairs of `identity.issuer`: then only a named operator's engine-audience token registers or retires trails on a running witness, and any other token the engine takes is `403` `not-an-operator`. Present and empty, no one administers the running witness, and `gateway witness register` and `retire` remain for a stopped one; absent, any token the engine takes administers, as in v0.11.0. Name the operators.
- **A front for other parties** passes `/witness/checkpoints`, the two reads under `/witness/trails/{trail}/` and now `GET /publickey`, which a reader with no pinned key holds to the named `keyId`; the two administration endpoints stay on the operator's side, with the list or without it (SECURITY.md; SPEC.md §6).
- **To read a witness from a record**, `gateway witness fetch` takes the store, registry, authority and public key as `gateway verify` does, the record, and a pin of the witness's key or none, and hands the runtime a key file, the statements and the head. It needs a runtime whose `audit verify` takes `--witness` (0.30.0 or later); the engine image's runtime pin stays at 0.21.0, and moving it is an image release of its own. `--witness-resume` and `--witness-save` name directories, one continuation file per witness.
- **Know what the member establishes** (SPEC.md §1.2a; SECURITY.md): on a receipt that verifies under the operator's gateway key, the holder of that key, minting the receipt at `servedAt`, named this origin and key identifier for this trail. Not that the witness holds anything for the trail or registered it, that a checkpoint was submitted, that the chain is countersigned, that the origin serves the key named, that the witness is independent of the operator, or anything about the operator's honesty. A receipt without it establishes nothing, not that the trail has no witness. The reading is the runtime's report, given verbatim.

## Amend ADR-0013 for what shipped in v0.11.0 (#239, closes #216)

Documentation only: `docs/adr/0013-checkpoint-witness.md` is amended in place, in Desk ADR-0010's form, with dated paragraphs beside the text they change, the status line and the delivery table (rows 1 to 4 delivered); no existing sentence is rewritten. The points: the head rule (more than one index past every statement is `witness-head-unreached`; exactly one past joins the walk); `witnessedAt` order is the witness's rule, and readers hold it to its form only; the delivery's rule 3 was followed and refused, not a new key; storage runs on Unix only; what the sync tests hold, and do not; reads at 64 in flight and 30 s to write an answer, no rate; `register` needs `--config`, the commands act on a stopped witness, a running one registers and retires through the operator endpoints, and `repair` stays offline; `409 index-bound`; after a write failure every submission is refused, resubmissions included; a submitter's token names `witness.audience`, distinct from `identity.audience`, and the other endpoints and the MCP transport refuse it; the two operator endpoints, and the operators list that follows below.

## An operators list for the two administration endpoints (#240, closes #229)

- `witness.operators`: closed `{issuer, subject}` pairs, each of `identity.issuer`, none given twice, none among `submitters` (refused at configuration, since a submitter's token opens the witness and nothing else); parsed by the reader that parses `submitters`, so the two lists' sentences are alike.
- Both endpoints go through the one gate they already shared: after the token is verified under the engine's audience, its issuer and subject are held to the list where the list is given, before a byte of a body is read. `401` for no token, an invalid one or a submitter's, unchanged; `403` `not-an-operator`, "an operator named by witness.operators is required here", otherwise, with no `WWW-Authenticate`, the body unread and nothing written. SPEC.md §6, SECURITY.md, the engine-config design note and one amendment line in ADR-0013 say so.
- Tests (`go/witness_config_test.go`, `go/witness_admin_test.go`): the member present, present and empty, absent, and present without `submitters`, and its refusals case by case; on both acts, an unlisted engine token and any token under an empty list `403`, a listed subject in another case or with a suffix `403`, a listed submitter's engine token, its own witness token and a token naming both audiences `401`; a listed operator registers (the next submission is judged by it) and retires, and a submission refuses its token; with the list absent the existing administration tests pass unchanged and an unlisted engine token administers.
- What it establishes: where the list is given, each act takes a token the signer's other token-checked endpoints take, verified as they verify one, under the engine's audience, of an issuer and subject the list names. What it does not: tell an operator from a holder of the operator's credential; the signer's other endpoints, `/seal` among them, take any token the engine takes as before, so the front still passes only the submission, the two reads and `/publickey`.

## A receipt names the witness its trail is registered with (#241, #242; ADR-0014, part of #199)

#241 proposed the record and #242 builds it as accepted, all eleven questions answered as proposed: delivery rows 2 and 3 in one pull request, so the member was never merged unexercised. The issue closes when Desk's part lands.

- **The member.** On an action receipt, the witness of the trail of the record `/act` found, read from the bytes whose digest matched; on an acquisition receipt whose request names a configured `trail`; never on a seal. `origin` is `https`, or `http` at a loopback address written as one, with a lowercase host, an explicit port and nothing after, at most 2048 characters; `keyId` is §1.2's form; `trail` the trail. The time is `servedAt`; no token, subject or registration is in it. It is an optional signed member of version 3, as `action.policy` and `decision.recordBytes` are: changed or removed, `signature-mismatch`.
- **The verifier.** `gateway verify` and verify-ts hold the member to its form at §1.4 order 1 (`malformed`), report `decision-trail-mismatch` when an action's `witness.trail` is not its runtime evaluation record's `trail` (a compared record with no `trail` included; a record not compared is not held to it), and report `witnesses`: the distinct `{origin, keyId, trail}` triples named by receipts that passed the ladder. A seal carrying the member is read as naming nothing, since §4 step 2 loads a seal by its four signed members.
- **The reader.** `gateway witness fetch` takes the store-wide verdict and reads nothing unless it is `ok`; takes each named witness's key from its pin whose identifier is `keyId`, else from the origin's `/publickey` held to `keyId` and the key rule, the report saying which and never from the receipt; fetches the head and the statements a page at a time, within §8.7, and hands them to the runtime. Every read's answer is classified one way. No answer is unreachable: the runtime then reads the statements the reader kept, under its pin, with no head, historically, and nothing without a pin. A refusal of any read, the head or the first or a later statements page, is quoted with `at`, `status`, `reason` and `error`, nothing is credited and the runtime is not run. An answer outside its form (a head that is not exactly one statement line of §8.2's form, a page not of its form) is `answer-not-of-form`, kept apart from unreachable and read under the pin with no head. Another key served is `witness-key-not-named`, nothing read; a key served by `/publickey` is not a pin, so a witness unreachable after serving it reads nothing. A head of its form whose signature, key or trail is wrong goes to the runtime, whose finding it is. Two fixed sentences join the report: the operator's gateway named this witness for this trail under its own key at T, which is not a statement of the witness; and, for a served key, the reading is as independent of the operator as the operator's choice of witness.
- **The corpus.** Nine vectors under `corpus/v3/stores/` (31 to 40), signed with the published test seed by a script kept apart from the code, among them two witnesses for one trail and the member on a seal; the process contract compares `witnesses` as a set; a verifier that ignores the member is caught by the corpus (`go/teeth_test.go`).
- **The scenario** (CI job "a receipt names its witness · end to end", `go/witness_scenario_test.go`), ADR-0014 determination 6 step by step: a witness W of this commit's binary on loopback with a test issuer; the runtime makes the trail; an engine G of this code naming W in `witnessedBy` acquires for the trail; the runtime writes R citing it; the trail is registered at W and the checkpoint through R submitted; G acts on R and seals; `gateway verify` reports W for the trail; `gateway witness fetch` takes W's key from `/publickey`, matched to the named `keyId`, and the runtime reports countersigned through R, current, no `witness-*` finding; a saved continuation resumes through a directory. Negatives: a key W does not hold (`witness-key-not-named`; pinned, `witness-signature-invalid`); a second witness holding nothing for the trail (`404 unknown-trail` verbatim, nothing credited; W's statements under its key `witness-signature-invalid`); W stopped (unreachable; with W's key pinned, the kept statement reads historically, countersigned through R). The runtime is a second pin beside the Dockerfile's, `ghcr.io/judgment-pack/judgment-pack:0.30.0` taken by digest and its version checked; the scenario also passed locally against 0.31.0.
- Tests: `go/witness_named_test.go` (the origin form case by case; the configuration refusals; the own key refused before anything is made; an acquisition naming a configured trail, the refusals before any source runs, no member without a trail; the member inside the signed bytes; an action named by its record's trail and by nothing else; `decision-trail-mismatch`; `connect` keeps the member; the golden re-minting), `go/witness_fetch_test.go` (a witness over a real loopback listener, the runtime stood in: the pin or the served key held to `keyId`, a pin of another key unused, another key served, the refusal verbatim, unreachable with and without a pin and kept statements), `go/witness_fetch_answers_test.go` (the review's three probes over an in-memory transport, adopted as regressions), verify-ts `test/witness-member.test.ts` and the corpus test comparing `witnesses` (83 of 83).
- What it establishes, and what it does not: above, under "What an operator must do". Desk's part (ADR-0014 §5, delivery row 5: its managed gateway started with `--witnessed-by` from `witness.json`, `trail` on its acquisitions, the comparison with an external gateway's `witnesses`, the panel's triples) is not in this release.
